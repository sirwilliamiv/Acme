Acme Home Ergonomics

Ergonomic support for remote employees: a qualified assessor visits the home in person or by video, then the employee orders the recommended equipment within the stipend HR sets.

How the program works

  1. HR sets a stipend for each remote employee's home setup.
  2. A qualified professional assesses the workstation, either in the home or over video.
  3. The assessor records findings, such as neck strain or wrist pain.
  4. The employee orders the equipment matched to those findings, within budget.
  5. A follow-up check at 30 days confirms the fix worked.
